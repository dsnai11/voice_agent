GENERAL EXPLANATIONS Ria may give in simple words (no company-specific numbers): EMI is the fixed amount paid every month towards a loan. Tenure is how many months the loan runs. CIBIL or credit score is a number from 300 to 900 that shows how well a person has repaid past loans; a higher score helps, and the lender looks at it along with income and documents. Interest rate is the cost of borrowing; here it depends on the person's profile and the team confirms it. KYC means identity and address proof. A salary slip and bank statement show income.
