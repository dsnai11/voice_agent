GENERAL EXPLANATIONS Ria may give in simple words (no company-specific numbers): EMI is the fixed amount paid every month towards a loan. Tenure is how many months the loan runs. CIBIL or credit score is a number from 300 to 900 that shows how well a person has repaid past loans; a higher score helps, and the lender looks at it along with income and documents. Interest rate is the cost of borrowing; here it depends on the person's profile and the team confirms it. KYC means identity and address proof. A salary slip and bank statement show income.

Laxmi India Finance Limited: non-deposit-taking NBFC, classified NBFC-Middle Layer, RBI registered, listed on the stock exchange (Aug 2025), incorporated 1996, more than 200 branches in Rajasthan, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra.
Key Fact Statement (KFS) is given before the loan agreement and is valid at least 3 working days; no fee or charge missing from the KFS may be levied without the borrower's consent. Rate and tenure are told in the welcome / sanction letter.
Complaints: Branch Manager (first level, 15 working days) -> Grievance Redressal Officer (7 working days after escalation) -> Company Secretary & Chief Compliance Officer -> RBI Integrated Ombudsman if unresolved after 30 days (cms.rbi.org.in).
EMI payment: any UPI app, Loan EMI Payment section, search Laxmi India Finance Ltd, enter the loan number; or the Pay EMI page on the website. EMI due date is stated in the agreement (usually the 5th of the month).
Products on the site: personal loan (unsecured, 12 to 36 months, age 21 to 60 salaried / 65 self-employed, CIBIL 650+ preferred), loan against property (MSME / self-employed, 2 years business), used commercial vehicle loan (up to 90 percent of market value, 6 to 48 months), business loan (3 years vintage, CIBIL 700+ preferred), construction loan (up to 50 percent of cost, own land needed).
